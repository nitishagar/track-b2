Live demo: diff two timing reports in your browser.

The real otps engine, compiled to WebAssembly. Paste two reports (OpenSTA report_checks text or native STA JSON), pick files from disk, or load the inlined sample pair — the diff runs locally and renders the summary, top regressions, clusters, and raw JSON.

Offline: after this page loads, Diff makes zero network requests — your reports never leave the machine. One serving note: browsers block ES module imports on file://, so serve this page over http (e.g. python3 -m http.server in docs/) or use the Pages URL.
